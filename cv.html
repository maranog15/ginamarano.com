Research interests

Assessing the impacts of abiotic disturbances — mainly drought and climate extremes — and biotic disturbances — pests and management — on the dynamics of temperate forests, with a focus on growth and mortality processes at the stand and individual-tree scale. My work combines process-based and empirical forest models with long-term observational datasets and field silviculture, and is aimed at management scenarios and decision support that forest practitioners can actually use.
