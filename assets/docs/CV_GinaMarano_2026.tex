\section*{Profile}
{\small\color{inksoft}Forester and forest ecologist with a strong background in process-based modelling and silviculture. My work assesses how abiotic stress --- above all drought and climate extremes --- and biotic disturbances such as bark beetles and management interact and affect the growth, mortality and long-term dynamics of temperate forests. The goal is to (1) understand the processes leading to forest stress, (2) design management scenarios and simulations to assess their potential outcomes, and (3) support foresters with decisions grounded in both field and modelling expertise.}
